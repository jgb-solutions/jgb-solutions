## Project Overview

MP3Pam is a music discovery site: search any song, artist or album, from any country or genre, play the official preview, watch the music video, and go straight to where you can listen to the full track, buy it, or get tickets to a show. The home page shows the top songs worldwide and by country, from the United States and the United Kingdom to Brazil, Nigeria, Japan and South Korea.

MP3Pam first launched in 2022 as a site for sharing audio. In 2026 we rebuilt it from scratch around a simple rule: no audio is hosted on MP3Pam. Every preview comes from the platform that provides it, and every link goes to a licensed service.

## The Challenge

People want one place to find a song and every legal way to hear it, but music data is spread across many services, each with its own catalog, links and limits. The site had to pull it together quickly, match the same song across platforms, stay fast on phones, and never host or offer music it doesn't have the rights to.

## The Solution

We built MP3Pam with **TanStack Start** on **Cloudflare Workers**, with a **D1** database and **Drizzle**.

- **Charts by country:** the top songs worldwide and in 10 countries, from Deezer's charts, with a play button on every track.
- **Artist and release pages:** top songs, albums, music videos and where to listen, for each artist and each album or single.
- **Official previews and videos:** about 30 seconds of each song, streamed from the platform that provides it, and official music videos from YouTube.
- **Where to listen, buy or get tickets:** links to streaming services, music stores and ticket sellers. Some are affiliate links, and the site says so.
- **Fast and searchable:** pages are rendered on the server and cached at the edge, so they load quickly and search engines can read them.
