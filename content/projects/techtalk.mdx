## Project Overview

Tech Talk (Tech Talk: An N Pale Teknoloji, "Let's talk technology") is a podcast about technology in Haitian Creole. Each episode brings guests and panelists together on a topic, from Starlink and credit cards to social media management and artificial intelligence, along with open-mic episodes where the panel takes on the news.

The website is the podcast's home: the latest episodes, a page for each episode and each panelist, and links to listen on Spotify, Amazon Music, Apple Podcasts, iHeartRadio and YouTube.

## The Challenge

The podcast needed a simple, fast site in Creole that puts the episodes first, works well on phones, and points listeners to the platforms they already use, without a database or a back office to maintain.

## The Solution

We built the site with **React** and **TanStack Start**.

- **Static and fast:** every page is generated ahead of time, so the site loads quickly and costs almost nothing to run.
- **Episodes first:** the home page lists the latest episodes, and each episode and each panelist has its own page.
- **Listen anywhere:** links to Spotify, Amazon Music, Apple Podcasts, iHeartRadio and YouTube on every page.
- **In Creole:** the whole site is written in Haitian Creole, the podcast's language.
