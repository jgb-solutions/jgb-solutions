## Project Overview

Cinetta is a guide to movies and TV shows. Visitors can search for titles and people, browse trending and chart lists, see new and upcoming releases, check the release calendar, watch official trailers, and find streaming services or nearby cinemas. The site also groups popular titles by language, genre, decade, country and streaming service, and lets visitors keep a personal list.

It's the movies and TV version of Musivo: one place to find something to watch and the legal ways to watch it.

## The Challenge

Movie and TV information comes from sources with different coverage, while titles and people can be easy to confuse. Cinetta combines catalog details with release, trailer and availability information so visitors can identify a title and find a way to watch it.

## The Solution

We built Cinetta with **TanStack Start** and **React**, using **TypeScript**, **Tailwind CSS**, **Cloudflare Workers**, **D1**, **Drizzle** and **oRPC**.

- **Catalog pages:** details for movies, shows, seasons, episodes and people, including cast, crew and viewing options.
- **Discovery:** trending and top charts, new releases, coming soon, genre and language lists, streaming services, free-to-watch catalogs and a release calendar.
- **Trailers and availability:** official trailers play through YouTube; watch links go to the services that offer a title, with availability varying by country.
- **Personal list:** visitors can collect titles in My List.
- **Server-rendered pages:** pages are rendered for visitors and search engines, with catalog data stored in D1.
