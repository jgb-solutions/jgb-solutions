## Project Overview

JGB Tools is a collection of free online tools for small, everyday tasks: messaging a phone number without saving it, checking whether a website is down, making QR codes, generating strong passwords, compressing photos and more. The idea is simple: tools that open instantly, do one job well and respect the people using them. The site is free to use and supported by ads. There is no account to create.

## The Challenge

Most "free tool" websites are slow, cluttered and built to collect data. Pages jump around while ads load, simple tasks hide behind sign-up walls, and it is often unclear what happens to the text, numbers or files you paste in.

We wanted the opposite: pages that load fast on a cheap phone, plain-language explanations of what each tool does with your data, and a professional look and feel across every tool.

## The Solution

We built JGB Tools with **TanStack Start** on **Cloudflare Workers**, with a **D1** database and **Drizzle**, and a shared core library for the tools' logic and the brand.

The tools available today:

- **Text Anyone:** start a WhatsApp, Telegram, Signal, SMS or Viber chat with any number without adding it to your contacts.
- **Is It Down?:** check whether a website is down for everyone or just for you, with its response time and your recent checks.
- **QR Code Generator:** QR codes for links, text, Wi-Fi, WhatsApp, email, phone numbers and contact cards, with an optional logo, made on your device and downloadable as PNG or SVG.
- **YouTube Thumbnail Downloader:** get any video's thumbnail in every size, up to HD.
- **Password Generator:** strong passwords and memorable passphrases, created on your device.
- **What's My IP:** see your public IP address and approximate location.
- **Image Compressor:** shrink and resize JPEG, PNG and WebP images in your browser, without uploading them.
- **Link Preview Checker:** see how a link will look when shared on WhatsApp, Facebook, X, LinkedIn and Google, with any problems to fix.

Most tools run entirely in the browser, so what you enter never reaches our servers. The few that need a server (such as Is It Down? and Link Preview) keep only what is needed and say so in the privacy policy. Every tool has its own guide.
