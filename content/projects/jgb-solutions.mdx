## Project Overview

This is the website of JGB Solutions, a web and mobile studio in Laval, Québec. It presents what the studio sells (websites, mobile apps and social media management), the products it builds and runs itself, such as JGB Tools and Street Brawl, and the work it has done for clients.

The site started as a personal portfolio. In 2026 it was redesigned to present JGB Solutions as a business: it speaks as "we", each service has its own page, and the studio's own products sit next to its client work.

## The Challenge

The site had to look like a company, not a freelancer, and make it easy to start a project, without a contact form that attracts spam. It also had to stay fast, be easy for search engines to understand, and be simple to keep up to date as new projects launch.

## The Solution

We built it with **TanStack Start**, so every page is rendered on the server and loads quickly, and gave it a bold orange, black and white design based on the JGB Solutions logo.

- **Design:** the logo's orange with black type, square shapes and the Saira typeface from the logo. Orange is used for highlights and buttons, while longer text sits on white or black for easy reading.
- **Contact without forms:** every page ends with a contact band offering email and WhatsApp, so visitors can reach the studio directly.
- **Content:** each project is a single MDX file. Services and products are listed in code, so a new service appears on the site, in the menu and in the sitemap as soon as it's switched on.
- **Search and sharing:** structured data describes the business to search engines, and the share image, favicons and logo are generated from the vector logo.
