## Project Overview

Emojiverse is an emoji website for everyone: search 1,923 emoji by name or keyword, and copy any of them in one click. Emoji are grouped in nine categories, from smileys and people to food, travel, objects, symbols and flags.

Every emoji has its own page with a plain-English meaning, the words people search it by, related emoji, its skin tones, the Emoji version it came out in, and its codes for developers (Unicode, HTML, CSS, JavaScript and URL).

## The Challenge

Many emoji sites are slow and thin on explanations, and some show emoji images they don't have the rights to. Emojiverse had to be fast on any phone, explain every emoji clearly, and use only data and images it's allowed to use, while staying cheap to run as a free site.

## The Solution

We built Emojiverse with **TanStack Start**, prerendered to static pages and served from **Cloudflare**, so every page loads instantly with no database or server to run.

- **Open data:** names and keywords come from Unicode's CLDR, and every image is Google's Noto Emoji (Apache 2.0), including skin tones and country flags.
- **One-click copy:** click any emoji, in a grid or on its page, to copy it to the clipboard.
- **A page per emoji:** its meaning, keywords, related emoji and developer codes, each with its own title and description so people find it from search engines.
- **Light and fast:** pages are plain HTML with small optimized images, and the site needs no account.
