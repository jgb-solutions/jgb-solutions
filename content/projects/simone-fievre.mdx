## Project Overview

Simone Fievre is a plus-size model, fashion designer, cosmetologist, nail artist, braider and makeup artist. Her website, built around her motto "Sois une marque dans ta vie" ("Be a brand in your own life"), brings her work, her shops and her writing together in one place.

## The Challenge

She needed a single site to present her biography and her different trades, sell products from her two brands, show her work in photo galleries and publish articles for her audience, all in French and easy for her to update.

## The Solution

We built the site on **WordPress** with **WooCommerce** for the shop:

- **Biography:** her story and the services she offers.
- **Shop:** two product lines, Sim Beauty Shop and Sim Creation Haiti.
- **Galleries:** separate galleries for Sim Creation, Sim Beauty and her own work.
- **Blog:** articles on beauty, style and personal care, published since 2022, for example on choosing clothing colors that suit you, timeless wardrobe pieces and the difference between perfume and eau de toilette.
- **Contact:** a contact page, WhatsApp chat and a PayPal donation option.
