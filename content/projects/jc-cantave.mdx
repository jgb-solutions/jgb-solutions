## Project Overview

JC Cantave provides aerial photography, drone video, promotional video, sports photography, imaging and Geographic Information Systems (GIS) services in Haiti, from Delmas, Port-au-Prince. The website presents the company and each of its services, shows its work in a gallery and a portfolio, and lets businesses and organizations ask for a quote.

## The Challenge

We first built the site in 2025 with **TanStack Start** and a **Strapi** CMS. It worked, but it needed a separate CMS server, the client couldn't easily add new projects, and the projects section had no real work in it yet. For 2026 the site needed to be simple for the client to update, cheaper to run, and ready to grow with new sections as real projects come in.

## The Solution

We rebuilt the site in 2026 on a **custom WordPress theme** hosted on Hostinger behind Cloudflare, with the design locked in and everything else editable from the WordPress admin.

- **No plugins to maintain:** the hero slider, services, gallery with a lightbox, portfolio, contact form, SEO and security settings are built into the theme, with one settings page for the logo, contact details, social links and sections.
- **A page for each service:** aerial photography, aerial filming, promotional video, sports photography, imaging and GIS, and graphic design, each with its own page.
- **French and English:** built into the theme, with every page, menu and list available in both languages.
- **Ready to grow:** a portfolio, team, drones and equipment, testimonials and a blog, each switched on from the settings when the client has real content for it.
- **Safe and searchable:** a fresh, hardened install replaced an unused, infected one on the same hosting; every page has its own title, description and canonical URL, and the old event pages redirect to the portfolio.
