## Project Overview

Top Facts is a directory of ranked lists across categories including technology, video and music, people and society, books and knowledge, earth and nature, the built world, games and space. Visitors can search and browse lists, see current entries and ranking changes, and follow lists to receive notifications when their rankings change.

Each list identifies its source and ranking method and shows when its data was last updated. Sources include public datasets, charts and catalogs such as the World Bank, Wikipedia pageviews, Wikidata, Apple charts, GitHub, Steam and Open Library, as well as facts verified by hand.

## The Challenge

Ranked lists can become stale or present opinion as fact. Top Facts ties entries to identified sources and ranking methods, then updates supported lists as their underlying data changes.

## The Solution

We built Top Facts with **TanStack Start** and **React**, using **TypeScript**, **Tailwind CSS**, **Cloudflare Workers**, **D1**, **Drizzle** and **oRPC**.

- **Source-based rankings:** data sources are connected through adapters and validated before changing a ranking; lists that cannot be ranked from supported data are not published.
- **Scheduled updates:** background jobs refresh lists and record recent moves and new entries.
- **What's new and following:** visitors can review changes, use RSS feeds and follow lists for notifications when rankings change.
- **List pages:** each list shows its entries, source, ranking method and latest update; share cards summarize the leading entries.
- **Privacy-conscious analytics:** page views are counted without cookies or personal data.
