## Project Overview

Anboudou Créations makes handcrafted fashion accessories: wallets, clutches, handbags, necklaces, bracelets and sandals, made by hand with beads, leather and other materials, in designs inspired by Haitian and African heritage. The business was founded in Port-au-Prince and is now registered in Quebec. We built its online store on Shopify, so customers in Canada and beyond can browse the collections and order directly.

## The Challenge

Every piece is handmade, so the store had to show the work up close and make each product easy to find. Customers in Quebec expect to shop in French, and others in English. The store also needed clear answers on payment, shipping and returns before anyone places an order, and a way to bring visitors back.

## The Solution

- **Six collections:** handbags, clutches, wallets, necklaces, bracelets and sandals, each with its own page, and product pages with several photos per piece.
- **French and English:** the store is available in both languages.
- **Payments:** customers pay by Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover or PayPal, and with Shop Pay, Apple Pay or Google Pay where their device supports it.
- **Shipping and policies:** shipping across Canada, free on orders over $100, and to the United States, with shipping, refund and privacy policies and a FAQ that answers questions about materials, delivery times and returns.
- **Customers who come back:** a newsletter signup offers 10% off the first order, and a contact form and phone number are on every page.
