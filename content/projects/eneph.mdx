## Project Overview

Enfants Épanouis d'Haïti ("Thriving Children of Haiti"), or ENEPH, is a non-profit social and educational organization founded on April 14, 2018. Its founders had worked for years on children's causes, many of them trained on the UN Convention on the Rights of the Child. ENEPH promotes children's rights, invests in their education and helps them develop their talents.

## The Challenge

ENEPH needed a website to explain who they are and what they do, show their programs and team, and keep supporters informed about their activities, in French for their audience in Haiti. The team had to be able to publish news and photos themselves.

## The Solution

We built the site on **WordPress**, so the team can post updates without a developer. It includes:

- **Programs and Objectives:** what ENEPH works on and why.
- **Team, Gallery and Contact:** the people behind the organization, photos of their activities and how to reach them.
- **Media and Blog:** news about their work, such as a first-aid training workshop for children (May 2021), the third edition of their "Haïti Voix d'enfants" ("Haiti, Children's Voices") program (November 2020) and the release of their video "Nou gen dwa" ("We have rights").
