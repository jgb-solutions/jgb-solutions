## Project Overview

Musivo helps people find songs, artists, albums and concerts. Its home page combines worldwide and country music charts with chart movements, popular artists and releases. Visitors can search the catalog, play platform-provided previews, watch official music videos, and follow links to streaming services, music stores and ticket sellers.

Musivo first launched in 2022 as MP3Pam, a site for sharing audio. In 2026 we rebuilt it from scratch around a simple rule: no audio is hosted on the site. It took its new name, Musivo, and its new address, musivo.app, in October 2026. Every preview comes from the platform that provides it, and every link goes to a licensed service.

## The Challenge

Music information and listening options are spread across services with separate catalogs and links. Musivo brings charts, artist and release pages, videos, concerts and listening options together while keeping audio on the platforms that provide it.

## The Solution

We built Musivo with **TanStack Start** and **React**, using **TypeScript**, **Tailwind CSS**, **Cloudflare Workers**, **D1**, **Drizzle** and **oRPC**.

- **Charts and discovery:** worldwide and country charts, chart changes, new releases, genres, trending artists and music videos.
- **Music pages:** songs, artists and releases link related music, videos and available listening options.
- **Concerts:** browse upcoming shows and ticket links.
- **Platform playback:** previews and videos are supplied by music and video platforms; Musivo does not host audio.
- **Server-rendered pages:** product pages are rendered for visitors and search engines, with data stored in D1.
