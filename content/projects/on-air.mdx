## Project Overview

OnAir is a directory of live TV channels, radio stations and podcasts from around the world. Visitors can browse live TV, radio and trending podcasts; explore channels by country, language or category; and search the catalog. Supported streams and podcast episodes play in the browser.

OnAir does not host or rebroadcast streams. Playback comes from the broadcasters and podcast providers. Its catalogs use data from iptv-org, Radio-Browser and Apple Podcasts.

## The Challenge

Public catalogs combine large numbers of channels and stations from different countries, and stream links can change or stop working. OnAir organizes this material into searchable TV and radio catalogs and provides browser playback where a stream is available.

## The Solution

We built OnAir with **TanStack Start** and **React**, using **TypeScript**, **Tailwind CSS**, **Cloudflare Workers**, **D1**, **Drizzle** and **HLS**.

- **Live TV and radio:** browse channel and station catalogs, with streams played from their providers in the browser.
- **Podcasts:** discover trending shows from Apple Podcasts and play available episodes.
- **Browse and search:** find media by name and browse TV and radio by country, language or category.
- **Catalog sources:** listings draw on iptv-org, Radio-Browser and Apple Podcasts.
